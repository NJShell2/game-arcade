🕹️ NEON ARCADE

🔵 Checkers

You are Red and move first. Full English draughts rules: captures are mandatory, multi-jumps must be completed, and a man reaching the far row is crowned king (its turn ends there). Win by taking every enemy piece or leaving the computer with no legal move. Draws are called automatically after 40 move-pairs with no capture or man move, or on threefold repetition. Win to climb a difficulty level; lose twice in a row and it eases off.
